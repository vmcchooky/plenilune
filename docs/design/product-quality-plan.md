# Plenilune product-quality plan

02/11/2026. Proposed acceptance criteria following owner feedback. This is a plan,
not a claim of qualification or an assessment of another company's products.

## Fixed direction

Light primary; cream/beige neutrals, tonal separation, muted olive accent. Flat
surfaces and neutral borders. Existing deterministic engine owns verdicts. AI may
propose accepted conditions or in-memory edits; no UI convenience implies acceptance
or device application. One edited line may change a large region. A witness is a
point example, never an equivalence proof.

## Three jobs, three information levels

| Job | First answer | Next action | Details on demand |
|---|---|---|---|
| Understand a change | What behavior changed, on which object, why | Inspect deciding source / choose another change | All groups, exact modeled regions, source mapping, limits |
| Check intended behavior | What must remain true, what may change | Review normalized conditions and scope; explicitly accept | Typed predicates, exceptions, full evidence |
| Resolve and hand over | What the candidate changes and what verification found | Human review decision; export/replay | Candidate edits, collateral, receipts and provenance |

Incomplete/unsupported/unanalyzed coverage is always visible at the relevant
information level. Compactness is never achieved by suppressing uncertainty.

## Sequence and exit gates

1. **Repair the task model.** Audit the complete existing journey against the three
   jobs above. Use five synthetic scenarios below, recording unnecessary steps,
   repeated statements, and missing next actions. Deliver an updated flow/state
   matrix and exact screen copy before new layout implementation.
2. **Choose a composition.** Produce three genuinely different compositions for
   the same single-change scenario using the agreed palette: compact change record,
   source change with inline consequence, and change list with focused detail.
   Compare reading order, density and action clarity. Review existing product
   captures and actual reference material, with provenance, without fabricating
   references or inventing numerical style measurements.
3. **Finish one representative screen.** Build the chosen composition with actual
   synthetic engine output, including multi-change and incomplete variants. Check
   desktop and mobile side by side. Freeze only after explicit owner approval.
4. **Extract and apply the system.** Shared shell/header, change record, source
   comparison, condition review, status, disclosure and feedback primitives. Use
   the approved screen for the rest of the journey. Replace superseded rules at
   their owner; remove dead styles instead of stacking another skin. Keep backend
   acceptance and receipt guards intact. Address inherited large UI components
   incrementally, with behavior checks for their real interaction boundaries.
5. **Validate understanding and finish.** Run target-user task trials, keyboard
   and screen-reader checks, four viewport checks, 200% zoom and all state variants.
   Complete screenshot-only critique, subtract redundant elements, then freeze
   reviewed screenshots for regression. Functional tests and axe are necessary,
   but they do not establish task understanding or owner taste approval.

Each stage produces reviewable evidence. Do not advance an unapproved composition
into all screens or call a self-assigned numeric score proof of quality.

## Required synthetic scenarios

- One added TCP/22 deny: name a concrete changed example and deciding rule, with
  the complete change groups available. Do not infer protocol intent from a port.
- Several objects and both newly denied/permitted behavior: reveal multiplicity
  and make selection obvious; do not present the first example as the whole result.
- EQUIVALENT within the model: explain what was compared without claiming device
  safety or intent fidelity.
- INCONCLUSIVE, partial, unsupported syntax or unmodeled configuration: uncertainty
  is visible before any optional detail; offer a relevant recovery action.
- Candidate repair, changed input and stale human decision: explain what must be
  reverified; acceptance never carries silently across revisions.

## Proposed usability acceptance targets

Targets below are hypotheses for trials, not measurements already achieved.

- After five seconds on the simple case, a target user can identify what changed,
  what decided it and the relevant next action.
- In at least four of five independent trials, the user completes the simple
  compare-and-inspect task without coaching. Record misinterpretations, wrong
  turns and time; do not treat a small trial as a population estimate.
- No participant mistakes an incomplete result for approval, a packet example for
  all affected traffic, or a review decision for applying a device configuration.
  Any such mistake blocks that design until corrected and retested.
- Intent verification presents relevant conditions and scope; the reviewer can
  explain what they accepted. All canonical definitions remain reviewable.
- Keyboard flow, visible focus, readable contrast, 200% zoom and 320/390/768/1440
  layouts work through the journey; zero serious/critical axe findings.
- Visual review compares typography, edges, spacing, controls, loading/empty/error
  states and recovery across screens. Owner approval and independent critique are
  recorded separately from automated results.
