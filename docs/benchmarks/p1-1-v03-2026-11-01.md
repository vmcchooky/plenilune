# P1-1 post-change — engine v0.3 (LCP residual) on the fixed corpus (measured 2026-11-01)

Post-change measurement for plan item P1-1, taken with the **same harness,
same corpus, same methodology** as the v0.2 baseline in
`p1-1-baseline-2026-11-01.md`, against the pre-registered gate in
`docs/plan/03-phase-1-engine-repair.md`. The only change between the two
runs is the engine: `acl-exact/v0.3` instead of `acl-exact/v0.2`.

## Environment and method

- Machine: Linux/amd64 VM, 4 vCPU Intel Xeon Platinum 8321HC @ 1.40 GHz,
  ~8 GB RAM, otherwise idle during the runs. `GOMAXPROCS=4`.
- Go 1.26.7. Engine under test: `acl-exact/v0.3` (`EngineVersion`).
- Default limits: 2 s / 2,000,000 operations / 50,000 boxes — unchanged.
- Corpus and harness: unchanged from the baseline run
  (`internal/verify/p11_baseline_test.go`, 5 runs per case). Ops and
  verdicts were identical across both passes taken (pass 1 below, pass 2
  in `p1-1-v03-2026-11-01-run2.json`); **ops is the primary metric**, wall
  medians are this VM's only.
- Raw data: `p1-1-v03-2026-11-01.json` (pass 1) and
  `p1-1-v03-2026-11-01-run2.json` (pass 2) beside this file.

## Evaluation against the pre-registered P1-1 gate

- **Identical-512 completes — PASS.** EQUIVALENT at 1,733 ops / 577 peak
  boxes under the unchanged default caps (v0.2: INCONCLUSIVE, stop=boxes,
  261,044 ops). Bonus completions outside the gate's letter:
  change_last at 512 (2,819 ops) and the 511→512 append (2,802 ops) also
  complete now; both were INCONCLUSIVE under v0.2.
- **Late-change/append: ≥50% ops and median-time reduction — PASS.**
  append_rule: 589 ops @100 (target ≤48,567; −99.4%) and 1,373 @250
  (target ≤232,272; −99.7%); median time 32.0→0.5 ms and 125.2→1.1 ms.
  change_last: 589 @100 (target ≤48,532) and 1,901 @250 (target
  ≤232,426); median time 31.1→0.5 ms and 128.9→1.1 ms.
- **Insert-at-head: no more than 10% worse than v0.2 — PASS, exactly
  equal.** 105,291 ops @100 and 508,952 @250, byte-identical accounting:
  an insert at the head leaves no common prefix, and the v0.3 construction
  degenerates to the v0.2 computation rather than paying alignment
  overhead. shadow_all (also no common prefix) is likewise unchanged.
- **No case that completed under v0.2 got slower in ops.** swap_adjacent
  and change_port improve at 100/250 (their shared prefixes are ~half and
  ~most of the ACL respectively).

## Remaining limits (recorded, not hidden)

- **swap_adjacent and change_port at 512 rules still end INCONCLUSIVE
  (stop=boxes).** Their residuals still contain wide rules whose own
  per-rule effective-region computation fragments past the box cap — the
  same wall v0.2 hits, reached through the residual instead of the full
  list. LCP helps exactly when the change sits behind a long shared
  prefix; it does not reduce the cost of a residual that is itself large
  and fragmented. swap_adjacent at 512 spends *more* ops than v0.2 before
  stopping (344,430 vs 261,044) because the prefix work is skipped and the
  residual construction proceeds further before the same wall; the outcome
  (INCONCLUSIVE, stop=boxes) is unchanged and no verdict is overstated.
- **Obligations over very wide regions on very large ACLs** are evaluated
  against a permit cover built per condition region; for a whole-space
  region on a ~512-rule ACL that construction is the same order of work as
  v0.2's permit-set build and can still exhaust the box budget, leaving
  the obligation INCONCLUSIVE while the verdict completes. This is
  strictly more than v0.2 delivers there (its verdict is INCONCLUSIVE in
  that territory) and never upgrades an outcome.
- No suffix alignment: only the common prefix is aligned (the plan item
  specifies prefix; insert-at-head is carried by the degenerate path
  above, at exactly v0.2 cost).
